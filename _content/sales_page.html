A salesperson's day

One handheld for the whole round.

The salesperson starts the day with a personal code and works through the route. Everything they do is tied to the shop they are standing in.

A route that shows what is done, what is next and what was missed

The day route lists every shop as done, in progress or to do, and opens directions to the next one in Google Maps. A week plan and a list of missed shops keep the round honest.

  • Four visit outcomes: order taken, no order, closed or other work, plus a note of up to 300 characters.
  • Salespeople are not tracked. Location is read only when the salesperson taps arrival or departure, to confirm they are at the shop. We keep whether they were at the shop, near, far or unknown. The only position saved is the shop's own pin, set on its first visit.
  • Directions go to the shop's pin. The salesperson's position is never put in a link.
  • The shop radius is yours to set, from 25 m to 1,000 m.

Special prices, approved by the right person

A salesperson proposes a special price on the handheld and confirms it with a personal code. If it is inside the limit you set, it is approved at once. If not, it waits for you or the office.

  • You set the approval route and a hard maximum discount.
  • The owner or office decides in a Sign-off queue, with their own personal code.
  • Nobody approves their own offer, and offers expire.
  • ProInsights Sales never changes a price in your ERP. The offer lives here, and your order system stays the master.

Money received, with a printed receipt in the customer's hand

When a customer pays on the spot, the salesperson records it on the handheld and prints a numbered receipt on a 58 mm roll. The receipt carries your own name and logo.

  • Record cash, cheque, card or transfer. A cheque needs its number and bank.
  • A hard maximum amount per receipt, agreed when your account is set up.
  • Every receipt is numbered. A void needs a reason, so a receipt never just disappears.
  • It is a receipt for money collected, not a tax document, and it says so in Greek. It records a payment; it does not process cards.

Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

Does ProInsights Sales replace our order system?

No. It works next to the order system you already use, and orders are still entered there. In ProInsights Sales the salesperson records the outcome of each visit: order taken, no order, closed or other work. The sales that follow show up in your numbers when you import your next ERP export.

Does it change prices or documents in our ERP?

Never. A special price is an offer that you approve inside ProInsights Sales. The reference is the customer's last invoiced net unit price, and nothing is written back to your ERP.

Do you track my salespeople all day?

No. Salespeople are not tracked, and there is no trail of where they have been. Location is read only when a salesperson taps arrival or departure on an open day, and only after they have acknowledged the GPS notice, to confirm they are at the shop. What is kept is a band (at the shop, near, far or unknown), the distance to the shop pin and the accuracy of the reading. The only position saved is the shop's own map pin: on a shop's first visit, check-in sets the pin from the salesperson's position, and that change is logged. A weak signal counts as unknown, never as far.

How does our sales data get in?

Export customers, products, salespeople, sales documents and targets from your ERP as Excel or CSV and load them with the mapping wizard. Rows that cannot be accepted are listed before anything is sent, with a CSV of the refused rows to fix. The screens show the date of your latest data, so you always know how fresh the numbers are. It is a file import, not a live link to your ERP.

Which devices does it run on?

Salespeople work on a phone or an Android handheld. Receipts print on a 58 mm roll from the Sunmi handheld through Chrome. The office works in a web browser on a computer.

Is the receipt a tax document?

No. It is a receipt for money collected. It carries your company's own name and logo and a signature line, and it states in Greek that it is not a tax document. ProInsights Sales records the payment; it does not process card payments.

Who can see what?

There are four roles: owner, office, salesperson and viewer. Owner, office and viewer read all customers, and only owner and office import data. A salesperson sees only their own customers, on the phone. The database enforces the roles, so a hidden button is not the only barrier.
