Questions

Before you ask.

How long does setup take?

One fifteen-minute onboarding call. You give us your Google, TripAdvisor and Booking.com pages, and your first daily report lands the next morning. There is no software for your team to install or learn.

Which platforms do you cover?

Google, TripAdvisor, Booking.com, Expedia, TheFork and Trip.com — six review portals on every plan, tuned to your market.

What languages do you support?

Briefings are delivered in English or Greek. AI-drafted replies are multilingual — each reply is drafted in the language the guest wrote in, in your house voice.

Do you post replies automatically?

No. Every reply is a draft — you review, edit and post. Sensitive content such as food-safety mentions or legal threats is never auto-drafted. You stay in control of every public word.

How fast do I hear about a 1-star review?

On the Pro plan, a 1-star review triggers a WhatsApp and email alert within 90 minutes, with a draft reply attached.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Plans are monthly, you can cancel anytime, and there is a 30-day money-back guarantee. The first report arrives within 24 hours of onboarding.
